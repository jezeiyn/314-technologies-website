Privacy notice

Talent network, Three One Four Technologies L.L.C. Version 2026-10-draft.

Who we are

Three One Four Technologies L.L.C ("314", "we") is a technology consultancy based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We run the talent network and decide how your information is used. You can reach us at [insert contact email].

What we collect

Only what you enter in the application form: your name and contact details, location, current role and employer, experience, skills and certifications, the kinds of work you would consider, your availability, and your rate range. We also keep our own notes after speaking to you, such as screening notes and whether we have verified a certification. We do not ask for identity documents, date of birth, nationality, health information or anything about your family.

Why we use it

To decide whether you fit current or upcoming projects, to contact you about them, and to propose suitable people to clients. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing and we do not sell it.

Who sees it

Only authorised 314 staff see your full profile. If you tick the optional box, we may show clients an anonymised summary of your skills, experience and region. Your name, contact details and employer are only shared with a client after you agree to that specific opportunity. Your private notes and internal ratings are never shown to clients.

Our systems are hosted with Supabase, which stores data on our behalf in [insert hosting region once chosen].

How long we keep it

We ask you to confirm your details once a year. If you do not respond and have not been in contact with us for 24 months, we delete or anonymise your profile. [Confirm retention period with legal adviser.]

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, stop using it, or delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to [insert contact email] and we will act on it within 30 days. Deleting your profile also removes the notes we hold about you; we keep only a one-way fingerprint of your email address so we know a deletion request was honoured.

The law that applies

We handle personal data in line with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data. If you live elsewhere, such as South Africa, Kenya or the European Union, we also respect the rules that apply to you. [Legal adviser to confirm wording and any registration requirements.]
