About Us

About 314 Technologies.

314 Technologies is a global technology and advisory firm. We build the infrastructure, systems, and platforms that organisations depend on, and we stay until they work.

Our Journey

Built from the inside out.

The founding team brings together experience spanning global technology delivery, public policy, government advisory, and senior executive roles at organisations including Google, Cisco, Boston Consulting Group, and national institutions across the Gulf region.

314 was built on a simple observation: the firms that won advisory mandates rarely stayed to build the thing. And the firms that stayed to build rarely understood the strategic layer well enough to get the architecture right. 314 is the answer to that gap.

The founding team has operated at the intersection of strategy and delivery long enough to know that the two cannot be separated. That conviction is the foundation every engagement is built on.

Our Team

Practitioners, not presenters.

314 is built around a team of practitioners with deep, hands-on experience across government and enterprise delivery. Every person we bring to an engagement has done the work before, not just advised on it.

Digital Public Infrastructure.
Delivered at Scale.

When governments, enterprises, and development organisations need to move from manual processes and fragmented records to scalable digital services, 314 DPI provides the infrastructure layer that makes it possible. Contact us

Citizen Service Portals

Always-on digital platforms enabling residents and businesses to access government services, lodge requests, and make secure payments from any device. Paper-based processes eliminated at the source.

Digital Identity and Authentication

Secure, interoperable identity verification across all government touchpoints. Built on national ID infrastructure with multi-channel access, reducing identity fraud and service friction simultaneously.

Functional Registries

Single, authoritative digital records for births, businesses, land, licences, and more. Real-time data sharing across entities removes duplication and eliminates manual reconciliation.
